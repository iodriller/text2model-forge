# Community code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces professional, specific,
and safe. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing,
credential disclosure, and deliberate disruption are not accepted.

Disagreement about model quality, licensing, architecture, or evidence is
expected. Address the work and provide reproducible evidence; do not attack the
person. Generated content used in reports must follow the relevant model terms
and may not expose private or proprietary input.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this
policy is violated. For an urgent private report, contact the maintainer through
the private reporting channel named in `SECURITY.md`. GitHub-wide abuse may also
be reported through GitHub Support.
